# Design System



This document outlines the core aesthetic and functional principles of our design system.



## Brand Identity



Our brand identity is built on a foundation of clarity, professionalism, and subtle vibrance.



### Colors



Our color palette is carefully selected to ensure accessibility, reinforce brand recognition, and create a consistent visual experience across all platforms.



- **Primary Color**: `#10B981` - This is our brand's most distinctive color, used for key interactive elements and calls to action.

- **Secondary Color**: `#3B82F6` - A supporting color for less prominent UI elements and secondary actions.

- **Tertiary Color**: `#F59E0B` - An accent color for highlights, badges, or decorative elements.

- **Neutral Color**: `#F8FAFC` - Our base color for backgrounds, surfaces, and non-chromatic elements.



The color mode for the theme is `light`.



### Typography



We use a thoughtfully curated selection of fonts to ensure readability and establish a distinct typographic hierarchy.



- **Headlines**: `manrope` - Used for all major headings to grab attention and convey importance.

- **Body Text**: `inter` - Chosen for its legibility and versatility in long-form content.

- **Labels**: `inter` - Applied to UI labels, buttons, and smaller textual elements for clarity.



### Shape and Form



The geometry of our UI elements contributes significantly to the overall feel of the product.



- **Roundedness**: `2` - A moderate level of roundedness (default) provides a friendly yet professional aesthetic to our UI components.



### Spacing



Consistent spacing is crucial for readability and creating a balanced layout.



- **Spacing**: `2` - A normal level of spacing (default) ensures elements have adequate breathing room without feeling too sparse or too dense.
